Analecta Stoa

Reading the
Ancient World & Medieval World

Welcome! This is my personal guide to the great works of the ancient and medieval worlds — a space I've put together for anyone curious about where our stories come from. You'll find full texts, clear summaries, key passages from the original texts, and notes to help you make sense of it all. By looking at how these foundational works were first written down and passed along, we can start to see surprising reflections of our own world today.
